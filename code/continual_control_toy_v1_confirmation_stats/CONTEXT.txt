SCIENTIFIC QUESTION
-------------------

This controlled construction tests whether erosion of an online structural
quantity can predict future performance failure earlier and more accurately
than ordinary performance monitors, and whether acting on that structural
warning improves cumulative reward.

This is an independent single-agent construction. It supports the proposed
mechanism in a no-reset continual-control setting; it is not evidence that the
peer-learning LBF or Overcooked conditions have been validated.

TASK AND SIGNAL
---------------

One uninterrupted scalar control stream follows

    x[t+1] = a*x[t] + b[t]*u[t] + w[t].

The actuator gain b[t] undergoes five seed-randomized, continuous
high-to-low-to-high drift-and-recovery events. Physical state, the online RLS
gain estimate, monitor state, controller state, and intervention state are
never reset at drift boundaries.

The registered structural quantity is estimated one-step robust viability
coverage over a fixed reference state interval and bounded disturbance set.
The operational risk score is one minus this estimated coverage. The true
latent actuator gain is used only for the oracle diagnostic, never by the
operational trigger.

DATA SEPARATION AND INFERENCE
-----------------------------

Development:

    32 complete streams, seeds 5000--5031.

Confirmation:

    64 untouched complete streams, seeds 0--63.

The strongest simple predictor selected on development was return_slope and
was frozen before confirmation. Confirmation did not retune the plant,
drift distribution, estimator, controllers, thresholds, prediction target,
or interventions.

The independent statistical unit is a complete uninterrupted seed stream.
The 95% confidence intervals use 10,000 percentile-bootstrap resamples of the
64 complete streams. Checkpoints, drift incidents, and time steps are not
treated as independent research replicates.

--------------------

Prediction:

    Frozen return-slope average precision          0.3551499

Both registered co-primary whole-stream confidence intervals have lower
bounds above zero. The intersection-union confirmation rule therefore passes.
All 64 streams contain five incident failures and five recoveries on average;
all registered confirmation and integrity gates pass.

INTERPRETATION
--------------

Within this preregistered controlled construction, online robust-viability
erosion is a substantially earlier and more accurate predictor of future
return failure than the strongest frozen performance baseline. Triggering the
backup controller from the structural certificate produces near-oracle reward
and materially outperforms return-triggered, never-switch, and
intervention-count-matched random controls.

The construction is deliberately aligned with the theory, so these results
establish a strong mechanism demonstration rather than universal external
validity. Peer-dependent validation must be established separately in LBF or
Overcooked.
