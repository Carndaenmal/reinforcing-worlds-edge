# LBF Track B V3 excluded-development protocol

Frozen before running pairing `5000` or discovery pairing `6000` on
2026-07-31. Every listed pairing is excluded from later confirmation.

Before any registered pairing was run, an unregistered engineering smoke
pairing (`999999`) showed that the original 52-checkpoint schedule did not
drive old-focal performance through the registered failure threshold. Hazard
and recovery blocks were therefore lengthened and the stream was frozen at 72
checkpoints. No registered pairing, threshold, endpoint, or contrast was
inspected or changed in making this amendment.

A second unregistered smoke pass established that unequal stochastic hazard
lengths could instantiate only one incident after recovery. Both registered
hazard blocks were therefore fixed at 12 learning checkpoints plus a
five-checkpoint plateau before any registered pairing was run.

The same engineering audit showed that a weak old-focal cue allowed return to
decline before the registered stable-performance precursor condition. The
fixed left-cue coefficient was strengthened from `0.5` to `1.5`; this changes
the task's intended response gap, not the structural or performance threshold.

V3 is a coordination-fork construction designed to instantiate, rather than
assume, the operational premises that failed in V2. It preserves the V2 record
and does not lower the `0.95` discovery or `0.90` membership thresholds.

The evolving peer is a feed-forward stochastic route policy updated by
sampled policy gradient. A fixed neutral focal probe waits at the fork, lets
the peer reveal its route, and follows it; this keeps probe success high while
estimating peer-only left-route coverage. The separately frozen old-left focal
immediately emits a left cue. Mild drift can therefore alter autonomous peer
behavior under the neutral probe while the peer still responds successfully
to the old focal. Continued drift eventually destroys that compatibility.
Continuation-partner identity is hidden until the fork, so all continuation
updates use the neutral cue. This prevents a universal partner-conditioned
policy from solving both conventions, which was the principal V2 failure.

The peer-only candidate is the registered nested sequence

```text
PEER_ENTER_LEFT_GATE, PEER_LEFT_READY, PEER_LOAD_LEFT
```

and must independently attain discovery coverage at least `0.95` on pairings
`6000..6003`. Monitoring pairings are `5000..5007`.

Continuation contains two seed-randomized right-learning blocks, two recovery
blocks, and an irrelevant style-learning block. The style block changes
generic policy/occupancy distances without changing the route core, providing
a nuisance-drift control. Schedule phase labels and latent peer parameters are
diagnostics and are unavailable to operational predictors or triggers.

The primary future target is a new downward crossing of held-out old-focal
success below `0.70` within three checkpoints, restricted to checkpoints that
are currently at risk. Monitoring and held-out rollouts are disjoint. The
independent inferential unit is the complete peer-learning pairing trajectory.

Development is eligible to freeze only if independent discovery, baseline
coverage, repeated incidents, recovery, structural precedence, nuisance
specificity, positive prediction direction, and positive mitigation direction
all pass. Development intervals are descriptive. No confirmation pairing is
launched automatically.
